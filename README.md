# GymGenie

**A personalized, equipment-aware workout recommendation system.**

GymGenie is a web application designed to make fitness more accessible and flexible by generating personalized workouts based on the equipment users have available, their fitness goals, and their workout preferences.

Whether you're working out at a fully equipped gym, traveling, or exercising at home, GymGenie aims to help you find effective exercises without being limited by your environment.

## Overview

Traditional workout plans often rely on specific machines or equipment, making them difficult to follow when access to those resources changes. GymGenie addresses this problem by recommending exercises that match a user's available equipment and individual preferences.

Users can customize their workouts based on:

- **Available Equipment:** Select the machines, weights, or other equipment currently accessible.
- **Target Muscle Groups:** Choose which muscle groups to focus on.
- **Experience Level:** Specify beginner, intermediate, or advanced.
- **Workout Duration:** Set the amount of time available for a workout.
- **Number of Exercises:** Choose how many exercises to include.

GymGenie is being developed to use information retrieval and ranking algorithms to identify relevant exercises and assemble personalized workout routines.

## How It Works

GymGenie is designed around an information retrieval pipeline that transforms user preferences into relevant exercise recommendations.

1. **User Input:** Users specify their equipment, target muscle groups, experience level, available time, and preferred number of exercises.
2. **Exercise Filtering:** The system identifies exercises compatible with the user's equipment and workout constraints.
3. **Information Retrieval:** Exercise descriptions and metadata are processed using TF-IDF vectorization.
4. **Relevance Ranking:** Cosine similarity and additional scoring criteria are used to rank exercises based on relevance.
5. **Workout Generation:** Highly ranked exercises are selected to create a personalized workout.
6. **Exercise Substitution:** Similar exercises can be recommended when equipment restrictions change.

The recommendation engine will combine text-based similarity with structured exercise information to produce relevant and practical workout recommendations.

## Recommendation Approach

GymGenie is being developed to explore multiple exercise recommendation and ranking methods.

**Rule-Based Ranking**

Exercises are scored using predefined criteria such as muscle group relevance, difficulty compatibility, and other workout preferences.

**TF-IDF and Cosine Similarity**

Exercise descriptions are represented as numerical vectors using Term Frequency–Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF). Cosine similarity measures how closely each exercise matches the user's query.

**Hybrid Ranking**

A combined approach incorporates text similarity and structured user preferences to produce more personalized recommendations.

These approaches will be compared to determine their effectiveness in retrieving relevant exercises.

## Evaluation

The recommendation system will be evaluated using test cases representing different workout scenarios, equipment combinations, and user preferences.

Evaluation will focus on:

- **Precision@K:** The proportion of recommended exercises that are relevant.
- **Recall@K:** The proportion of relevant exercises successfully retrieved.
- **NDCG@K:** The quality of exercise rankings based on graded relevance.
- **Constraint Satisfaction:** Whether recommendations respect equipment and workout restrictions.
- **Workout Feasibility:** Whether generated workouts meet the user's time and exercise-count requirements.

The goal is to evaluate both recommendation relevance and the practical usefulness of generated workouts.
